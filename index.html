The proposal in one view
04 / TWO ROUTES

Two delivery routes.
The same longer-term opportunity.

Both options use one WhatsApp Business API solution for guest conversations and consent-based re-engagement. BDGOAL designs and manages the roll-out for all seven restaurants; the distinction is how the initial notification is sent.

OPTION 01TWO CHANNELS

SMS + WhatsApp API

Send the initial booking confirmation or reminder by SMS. Include a WhatsApp link for guests who want to speak with the restaurant.

SMS: essential booking information
WhatsApp: guest replies and service
WhatsApp: opted-in marketing

Best fit when the group wants to separate notification costs from richer guest conversations.

OPTION 02ONE CHANNEL

WhatsApp API Only

Send the approved booking message directly through WhatsApp. Guests can reply in the same conversation without changing channels.

WhatsApp: booking notification
WhatsApp: guest replies and service
WhatsApp: opted-in marketing

Best fit when the group prefers a single guest-facing communication channel.

Both routes are implemented and coordinated by BDGOAL. Neither assumes a parallel WhatsApp Business App inbox or an automatic link to the reservation system.

Why this arrangement?

SMS handles the predictable notification. WhatsApp is used when the guest wants to interact or has actively chosen future updates.

The important limitation

An SMS WhatsApp link does not automatically create a WhatsApp conversation, prove consent or update SevenRooms. The guest must act; system synchronisation needs integration.

After marketing opt-in: segment eligible guests, send relevant approved WhatsApp marketing templates and offer a route to book again. SMS can also support lawful marketing; this design is about channel choice and two-way follow-up.

11 / CONSENT & GOVERNANCE

The guest stays in control.
So does Aqua.

The same system can support service and marketing, but they should remain distinct in both purpose and permission.

01

Service permission

Collect the appropriate contact permission for reservation notices. A guest messaging the restaurant opens a service conversation, not blanket consent for future promotions.

BOOKING COMMUNICATION
02

Marketing choice

Ask separately whether guests would like future restaurant news and offers. Keep the consent record, scope, timestamp and a clear opt-out route.

EXPLICIT OPT-IN
03

Minimal data use

Use the information needed to send and manage messages. Define staff permissions, retention, exports and offboarding before go-live.

GOVERNANCE
The commercially useful audience is not every past diner. It is the group of guests who have deliberately agreed to hear from the restaurant again.

WhatsApp requires approved business-initiated templates and opt-in practices consistent with local law. Template approval is not a substitute for obtaining consent.

Governance and continuity
12 / SECURITY & MIGRATION

Protect guest information.
Make the transition clear.

DATA SECURITY

Evidence, not assumptions

BDGOAL will coordinate the proposed WhatsApp API provider's security and privacy documentation for Aqua's review, including relevant certifications, the data-processing agreement, hosting location and access controls.

Account ownership, guest-data access, retention, permitted uses and exit procedures will be documented in the agreed service scope.

MIGRATION & VERIFICATION

Clear limits from the outset

Chat-history migration is supported within the confirmed technical scope. The contents and date range transferred depend on the source account and migration method.

Blue Tick verification cannot be assumed to carry over between WhatsApp Business App and API environments; any verification arrangements are assessed separately.

One WhatsApp operating environment

The proposal assumes a single WhatsApp API solution, not parallel WhatsApp inboxes. Automatic booking-data synchronisation is not included without an agreed integration scope.

Supplier assurance documents, migration prerequisites, hosting details and the data-processing agreement will be provided or verified as part of commercial and technical onboarding.
